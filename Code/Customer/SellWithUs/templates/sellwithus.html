Terms & Conditions
1. Account Creation and Submission
  • Users must create an account under the “Sell with Us” section to list their products.
  • A form must be submitted with accurate details, including name, email, contact number, pickup address, city (Rajkot), pincode, product details (name, brand, description, size, color, category), and images (front, back, side views). Submission of a valid bill for the product is mandatory for verification.
2. Product Approval
  • Submitted products will undergo review by the StyleSurfer admin team.
  • Admin has the sole discretion to approve or reject the product. The decision will be communicated to the user via email.
  • Approved products will go live on the website within 7-10 days.
3. Rental Process
  • Once a customer rents the listed product, the owner will be notified via email with customer details.
  • The owner will receive 50% of the rental price directly to their registered account.
4. Product Return
  • After the rental period, the rented product will be returned to the website. The website will then return the product to the original owner.
  • StyleSurfer is not liable for damages caused during the rental period but ensures necessary quality checks before return to the owner.
5. Owner Obligations
  • Owners must ensure that the listed products are genuine, of good quality, and accurately described.
  • Owners must provide valid bank account details for smooth payment processing.
  • Owners are responsible for any taxes or charges applicable to their earnings.
6. Payment Terms
  • Owners will receive their share of the rental income (50%) after the customer has successfully rented the product.
  • Payments are processed within 7 business days of a successful rental.
7. Liability and Damages
  • Minor repairable damages caused to products during rentals will be handled by StyleSurfer.
  • For irreparable damages or product loss, StyleSurfer’s liability is capped at 30% of the listed product price.
8. Confidentiality
  • All user data, including bank details and personal information, will be kept confidential and used only for payment processing and communication.
9. Exclusivity
  • Products listed on StyleSurfer should not simultaneously be listed on any other platform offering similar rental services.
10. Modifications
  • StyleSurfer reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Users will be notified of significant changes via email.
11. Notices
  • All official communications will be conducted through the registered email address provided during account creation.
